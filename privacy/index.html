Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026 · Prepared for the current private, locally operated application.

Luke Studio is a personal creator workspace operated by Hansen Hu. Its Google OAuth application is currently named Hansen YouTube Analytics for Muse. This policy describes the Google connections and local storage used by the application.

Information the application accesses

  • YouTube: with the account owner’s permission, channel identity, authorized channel analytics and estimated revenue. It also retrieves public metadata and statistics for channels the owner chooses to follow.
  • Google Calendar: the list of subscribed calendars and events in the connected primary calendar. The application creates and manages a separate Luke Studio calendar for scheduled work blocks. It does not request permission to edit the user’s other calendars.
  • Local workspace: scripts, references, tasks and manually maintained partnership records stored in the owner’s local workspace or Obsidian vault.
  • Email: the current website does not automatically read Gmail. Partnership records may contain information the owner manually enters or explicitly imports. A future Gmail connection would require a separate disclosure and authorization.

How information is used

Information is used to display channel performance, organize references, show calendar events and synchronize the owner’s scheduled work. Google data is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train general-purpose AI models. The application does not send emails, publish YouTube content or place financial trades.

Storage and retention

The working application currently runs on the owner’s computer. Google access and refresh credentials are stored in private files outside the website’s public files and outside the Obsidian vault. Authorized analytics snapshots and workspace records are stored locally. A private calendar cache supports incremental synchronization. The primary-calendar import window covers approximately the previous six months and next twelve months; incremental changes and managed work blocks may remain cached outside that window.

Public Following statistics use a rolling 30-day active cache. References explicitly saved to Watchlist, including captured public metadata, remain until the owner deletes those records and their backups. Channel analytics, tasks, scripts, partnership records and their backups remain until the owner removes them. Removing a visible item or disconnecting Google does not automatically remove stored snapshots or backups. The owner controls any independent Obsidian synchronization or backup services.

Sharing and external services

Calendar and YouTube requests are sent to Google to provide the connected features. Scheduled tasks are written to the dedicated Google calendar, where the owner’s Google sharing settings apply. The application does not independently sell or distribute Google user data to other organizations. It does not automatically send Google account data to an AI service.

The separate stock-price feature requests public quotes from Yahoo Finance without connecting a brokerage account. Visiting linked services is subject to their own privacy policies. The working Studio may load public fonts and media from external providers.

This public information site contains no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or Google account data. Its hosting provider may process technical access logs, such as IP addresses, under its own policies.

Disconnecting and deletion

The account owner can revoke Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future access when the application next checks its authorization; it does not erase local copies or the dedicated Google calendar.

To remove stored information, the owner can delete the relevant local records, snapshots, credential files and backups, and delete the dedicated calendar or its events in Google Calendar. For assistance identifying or deleting the relevant information, contact itshansenhu@gmail.com.

Security and changes

Google API communication uses HTTPS, and local credential files use restricted filesystem permissions. The working server currently listens only on the local loopback interface. These measures reduce access risks but do not guarantee absolute security. Any future hosted deployment or additional use of Google data will require updated disclosures and, where needed, fresh consent before that use begins.

Google API data use

Luke Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
